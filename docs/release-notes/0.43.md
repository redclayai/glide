## Added

**Ask — a one-off instruction for the selection in front of you.**

The toolbar now ends with **Ask…**. Click it, type what you want done — "make this sound less
annoyed", "turn this into a bulleted agenda", "say this in Spanish but keep the greeting" — and press
Return. The result appears with **Replace** and **Copy**, same as any other AI action.

Saved actions answer the things you want repeatedly. Ask answers the rest, which is most of them:
things wanted exactly once, with nothing to name and nothing to save.

## Fixed

**The panel no longer times out while you are using it.** Two rules were dismissing it out from under
the new field:

- The 15-second visibility cap fired regardless of what you were doing. Fifteen seconds is a long
  time for an unattended overlay and a very short time to compose a sentence. Interaction now defers
  the clock rather than disabling it — the moment the pointer leaves and nothing is running, it
  restarts.
- A rule that dismisses the panel when Glide holds focus was killing the instruction field one second
  after it opened, because a field you can type into *requires* that focus. It now only applies when
  the panel isn't waiting on you.
