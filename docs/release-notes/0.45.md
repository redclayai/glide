## Changed

**The rewrite panel is now a card.**

It was a single row of buttons that was rebuilt for every state, which is why it changed width —
and appeared to jump — each time it did anything. It is now a dark container with a white card
inset in it: a strip across the top, a row of tabs under that, and a body that changes underneath
while the rest stays put.

- **Tabs** across the header, one per action group, plus Suggested and More.
- **The instruction field is always visible**, with the placeholder "Enter your own…". The old
  Ask… button and the mode behind it are gone — a field you can see is a field you remember you
  have.
- **Results are shown as a diff**, not as replacement text: insertions highlighted in green,
  removals struck through in red, so you can see what changed without re-reading your own sentence.
- **"This text is well-written"** when a rewrite comes back identical, rather than a diff with
  nothing in it.

**Inter is now the card's typeface**, bundled with the app (SIL Open Font License), replacing the
system font.

Every colour and measurement in the card — the 37pt strip, the 4pt accent rule, `#2F7B70` headings,
the 146×26 instruction field — was measured from the reference rather than estimated.

**Selecting text now checks it, without being asked.**

The card used to open with a row of buttons and wait. It now opens with the correction already in
it — select a sentence and the spelling fix is there before you have finished reading the card,
with the grammar pass arriving a few seconds later and a "Checking grammar…" line saying so until
it does.

**Off by default.** Turn it on in Settings → General → "Check spelling and grammar as soon as I
select". Spelling is instant and offline; the grammar half runs the on-device model on every
selection, and that is enough of a cost that it should be something you choose rather than
something you discover.

**Spelling corrections are more careful**, which matters now that they happen uninvited. A word is
only corrected when the OS's two spelling engines agree on the replacement — they agree on real
typos and part company on words that are not typos at all. Measured over 23 misspellings: 17 fixed,
6 correctly left alone, none wrong. Among those left alone, `Supabase`, which one engine wanted to
replace with "Superbness".

**The card says when it is working.** A spinner and "Checking spelling…" / "Checking grammar…"
appear in its header for as long as the pass is running. Previously, when spelling found nothing
there was no diff to hang a progress line on, so the card showed its buttons and sat silent for
the several seconds the model took — which read as nothing happening rather than as working.

## Fixed

- Two dismiss buttons appeared on the card at once.
- The instruction field accumulated one layout constraint per selection.
- **Several typos close together were all ignored.** The OS sometimes reports them as one
  multi-word span, and Glide treated that as a single correction — skipping every misspelling
  behind it. "Teh meetng is tommorow." came back untouched.
- A selection poll four times a second could wipe a result, a spinner, or a correction you were
  half-way through reading.
- **The card kept proposing a change you had already applied.** Accepting a correction hid the
  card but left the old proposal inside it, so the next selection showed that instead of looking
  at the new text. The card's body now belongs to the selection it was computed for and is
  discarded the moment the selection changes.
- The card no longer re-opens on the text you just corrected.
- A card that dropped a long diff kept the diff's height, leaving the buttons stranded at the top
  of an empty panel.
- The card could appear in the corner of the screen instead of beside the text you selected.
- **Nonsense rewrites are no longer offered.** On text that needed no correction the on-device
  model tended to echo the input and keep going, so the card proposed your sentence twice. Model
  output for Grammar now goes through the same check the inline proofreader has always used, and
  a result that is not recognisably your sentence with its errors removed is discarded.
- "This text is well-written" now actually appears when nothing needs changing.
