## Fixed

**The toolbar no longer moves while you're using it.**

It re-anchored itself every few hundred milliseconds — under your pointer, mid-click, and out from
under an open menu. Two causes, both now fixed:

Glide checks whether the selection has changed by comparing what the app reports. Web and Electron
apps return the *same* selection with and without a trailing newline between one read and the next,
so an exact comparison saw a change constantly and repositioned the panel each time. The comparison
now ignores whitespace, which is precisely what differs between two reads of an unchanged selection.

And a visible panel now stays put while the pointer is over it or one of its menus is open. Even a
real selection change shouldn't move a toolbar you're reaching for — the system's own selection
callout stays where it is until dismissed.

## Changed

**The Actions pane was rebuilt.**

It was thirty-five identical rows with a checkbox column, a faint pin on every one, and no hierarchy
— a list you scan without reading. Now:

- **Sections.** The grouping already existed in the data and the toolbar already used it; the list
  simply wasn't reading it. Thirty-five rows became seven short ones, with your own actions first.
- **A second line per row** saying what the action will do — replaces your text, copies the result,
  opens a link, shows the answer. Naming the *kind* was the obvious first try and the wrong one: in
  the Writing and AI sections every row then read "AI", which is the heading repeated fifteen times.
- **Switches instead of checkboxes**, and icons in consistent tiles, because SF Symbols vary enough
  in optical weight that "Aa" and "scissors" don't line up on anything without one.
- **Pins appear on hover** or when set, rather than thirty-five permanent ghosts.
- **A real empty state**, and the global switches moved out of the detail pane — where they'd been
  stacked as a second form with its own background, reading as a different app — into a quiet footer
  strip that belongs to the pane.

Deliberately not borrowed from the web galleries that prompted this: gradient surfaces, display type,
card grids. This is a macOS Settings window, and what makes one look unfinished is missing hierarchy
and rhythm, not missing ornament.

See ADR-142.
