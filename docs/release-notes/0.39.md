## Fixed

**Tab now applies the correction the capsule is offering.**

When a spelling fix appeared with a "Tab" chip on it, pressing Tab could accept an autocomplete
suggestion instead — leaving your text unchanged and inserting a word you hadn't asked for.

A correction is only ever shown when nothing else is claiming the key. But the autocomplete pipeline
keeps running, and it re-offers a suggestion within the second it takes you to reach for Tab — and
then it won the key back. That race was guarded for AI grammar fixes and left open for spelling
fixes, which is why it looked arbitrary. Whatever is on screen offering Tab now gets Tab.

**The Tab chip no longer truncates to "T…".** Under a tight width the layout was taking space from
the chip rather than wrapping the sentence next to it, so the key you were being told to press was
rendered unreadable.

## Known

A spelling correction is still suppressed entirely while an autocomplete suggestion is visible — one
suggestion at a time. Since autocomplete fires often, that's why corrections appear only sometimes.
That's existing behaviour, not a change, but it's the reason the capsule is less frequent than the
typos are.
