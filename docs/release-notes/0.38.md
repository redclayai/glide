## Fixed

**The toolbar no longer walks down the screen when you use its dropdowns.**

Where an app exposes no usable rectangle for your selection, Glide anchors the toolbar to the pointer
instead — right for the first placement, since that's where you just finished dragging out the
selection. It was also re-anchoring there on every subsequent check, so reaching *into* one of the
panel's own menus moved the pointer downward and the panel followed it down, one step at a time.

A visible panel now never follows the pointer. It stays where it was placed.

**Dropdown menus open downward.** They were positioned from a point above the button's top edge, so a
menu opened over the toolbar rather than under the item it belongs to.
