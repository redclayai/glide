## Fixed

**Polish now works.** It never has. The button's handler was guarded by a test on the busy
indicator's `isHidden` property — but the busy state is driven by `startAnimation` and
`isDisplayedWhenStopped`, and `isHidden` is never set at all. The condition was therefore always
true, and every Polish click returned before firing the action. Grammar had no such guard, which is
why one worked and the other silently did nothing.

## Changed

**The selection toolbar looks like a system control now.**

It had a Glide glyph on the leading edge, two SF Symbols beside their own text labels, and raised
white pills inside a white capsule. Apple's text-selection callout — the one that appears over a
selection on both iOS and macOS — is a single capsule of flat text items divided by hairlines,
carrying no vendor glyph and no per-item background.

- The app mark is gone. A control that appears over your writing is furniture, not branding.
- The icons are gone. A label reading "Polish" is not clarified by a wand next to it, and the symbol
  standing in for Grammar was `checkmark.gobackward`, which means *revert*.
- Items are flat, at menu font size, with a quiet highlight on hover instead of a permanent raised
  fill — so nothing inside the capsule competes with the capsule for depth.
- Hairline dividers between every item, including before the dismiss button, which now reads as one
  more item in the row rather than something bolted to the end.
- The capsule is a true capsule, its radius derived from its height rather than fixed at 15pt.
